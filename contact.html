Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our services.

What industries do you serve? ▼

We serve manufacturing, energy & utilities, logistics, agriculture, smart buildings, water management, healthcare, and smart city projects. Our solutions are industry-agnostic and can be customized for any vertical requiring connected device management.

How long does a typical deployment take? ▼

Pilot deployments typically take 4-8 weeks. Full production deployments vary based on scope but typically range from 8-16 weeks. Our phased approach ensures you see value at each milestone while minimizing risk.

Do you offer ongoing support and maintenance? ▼

Yes! We offer 24/7 support with clear SLAs, proactive monitoring, regular updates, and ongoing optimization. Our support packages include device lifecycle management, security patches, feature updates, and dedicated technical account management.

Can you integrate with our existing systems? ▼

Absolutely. We specialize in integrating with existing ERP, MES, SCADA, PLCs, and analytics platforms. Our team has extensive experience with legacy systems and can build custom gateways, protocol converters, and APIs to ensure seamless data flow.

What security measures do you implement? ▼

Security is built into every layer: device identity management, end-to-end encryption (TLS/SSL), zero-trust network architecture, regular security audits, compliance with industry standards (ISO 27001, SOC 2), and secure firmware updates over-the-air.

What is your pricing model? ▼

We offer flexible pricing models including project-based fees for deployments, subscription-based pricing for platform services, and managed services with SLAs. Contact us for a customized quote based on your specific requirements and scale.
